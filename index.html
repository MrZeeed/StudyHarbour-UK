A little clarity before you take the next step.

Who is StudyHarbour UK for?

International students considering study in the UK, preparing to arrive, or already settling into student life.

Can I explore support before joining?

Yes. Open any student support card to explore a short starting checklist. You can browse these resources without joining.

Does this website submit university applications?

No. This website offers general starting points. Applications should be submitted through the university or an authorised application service.

Are entry requirements and deadlines the same everywhere?

No. They vary by university, course, intake and applicant. Always confirm current information on the university’s official website.

How do I join the community?

The community invitation link has not been added yet. The Join Our Community button shows its current availability.

Can you guarantee admission, accommodation or a job?

No. StudyHarbour UK does not guarantee offers, housing or employment. Decisions and availability depend on the relevant university, accommodation provider or employer.
